AI options

WoW Companion doesn't come with its own AI. It uses an AI account you already have, like Claude or ChatGPT, to answer your questions. Plans and map routes work without one. A free model running on your own PC works too.

Works without an AI: your plan, the numbered route on the map, the next-steps list, and the in-game panel with its gear upgrade and talent picks. Needs an AI: asking questions in the app.

The AI part answers your questions in plain English, like "What should I do next?" or "Is this quest worth it?". It reads the facts about your character and writes the answer for you.

Setting one up is optional. Without an AI helper, the app still makes your plan and lists your next steps, and the in-game panel and map pins still work. The only thing you lose is asking questions. The app tells you "Answers need an AI client" until you set one up. You can add one any time.

The choices

In the app, click the gear, open AI, and look at How should the companion answer?. It has three settings. Pick the one that matches what you already have.

  • An AI app on this PC. Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity. You sign in once, and it uses the account you already have.
  • An AI account with a key. Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or OpenRouter. You paste in an API key, which is a private password from the provider.
  • A model on this PC (free). Ollama or LM Studio. They run on your own computer, so there is no account and no bill.

Good to know

  • A subscription is not an API key. A Claude.ai or ChatGPT subscription can't be pasted in as a key. To use a subscription, pick Claude Code or Codex.
  • Keys bill your own account. The app doesn't charge you anything. Each answer can show a small line saying what it cost: turn on Show tokens used in Settings > AI. Your provider's console lets you set a spending limit.
  • Test it. For the key and local choices, press Test in Settings > AI. It sends one tiny message with none of your game data.
  • Your key stays on your PC. See Safe & private.
  • Don't see your AI app? Install it, sign in once, then press Re-scan. No restart is needed. The list also has an older Gemini CLI entry, which only works with a paid Gemini API key, and a Custom command entry for people who know what that is.
  • Answer length. Settings > AI also lets you choose Brief, Standard or Detailed answers.
