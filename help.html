Help and FAQ

Quick answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, tell us at the bottom.

In the game

The panel doesn't show

Type /wowc in the game chat. It opens and closes the panel. If nothing happens, check that WoW Companion is in the addon list at character select and is switched on.

If it isn't in the list, the game hasn't seen it yet. Fully quit the game and start it again: a new addon is only noticed at start. Also check that the WoWCompanion folder sits inside your game's Interface\AddOns folder. The app's setup checklist can put it there for you.

If the panel is somewhere strange, type /wowc reset.

"Advice from … ago" looks old

The advice is written by the app. If the app isn't running, nothing new is written, so the advice gets older. Open WoW Companion, then type /reload, wait a few seconds, and type /reload again. The first one saves what your character has. The app then writes fresh advice. The second one loads it.

Also check that Keep in-game advice up to date is on in Settings > Game.

/reload or a full restart?

Use /reload to refresh your plan. It's quick and does the job almost every time. Do a full quit and restart of the game the first time you install the addon, or whenever an update adds a new addon folder. The game only looks for new addons when it starts.

"What's new" keeps showing

Click Got it on the box. The game remembers that when it next saves, which happens when you /reload or log out. If you close the game another way before then, it can show again. You can also switch it off: type /wowc options and turn off Show What's new after an update.

Resizing and locking the panel

Drag the bottom-right corner of the in-game panel to resize it. Right-click that corner to go back to automatic size. To stop the panel moving or resizing by accident, type /wowc options and turn on Lock panel position. Turn it off again to resize.

The app has a small grip in its bottom corner, too. Drag it to resize the app's window.

In the app

The app says no AI is set up

The note says "Answers need an AI client". Your plan still works, but asking questions needs an AI helper. Click the gear, open AI, and pick one. If you've already installed an AI app, press Re-scan and sign in once. See AI options.

Windows blocked the installer

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's the SmartScreen check. It shows because the installer isn't code-signed, and small free projects often skip that. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer doesn't need administrator rights.

The app isn't over the game

In the game, open System, then Graphics, and set Display Mode to Windowed (Fullscreen). Nothing can draw above the game in true Fullscreen. If the app seems hidden, press Ctrl+Shift+W to show it.

How do I uninstall cleanly?

  1. If you saved an API key, open Settings > AI in the app and press Remove first.
  2. Quit the app. Then open Windows Settings, then Apps, find WoW Companion and click Uninstall. The uninstaller has an option to delete the app's own data too. Tick it to remove everything.
  3. To remove the addon, quit the game and delete the WoWCompanion and WoWCompanion_Digest folders from your game's Interface\AddOns folder.

Report a problem or say hello

Tell us what you tried, what happened and what you expected. Use the feedback form. It needs a free GitHub account. If you add a screenshot, crop or blur your account name, your character's name and other players' names first.
