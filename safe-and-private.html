Safe & private

Here's what WoW Companion does with your game and your data.

It only reads

  • The addon only shows things. It never casts spells, uses items, or sends chat, whispers or mail. It never buys, sells or bids. It never presses keys or moves your character. It uses only the game's own read-only information, and a test in its code fails the build if a forbidden action turns up.
  • The app never touches the game. It is an ordinary window beside or over the game. It doesn't inject anything into the game or send it any input.
  • Nothing is automated. You play. It tells you what it sees.

Your game data stays on your PC

The addon writes down what your character has: bags, gear, quests, talents, where your hearthstone is bound, and the trainers, vendors and gathering spots you visit. The game saves that to a file on your own PC, and the app reads it from there. The addon sends nothing out of the game.

What does leave your PC

  • Your questions go to the AI you chose. When you ask, your question and the facts about your character it needs to answer go to the AI helper you picked: your AI app's own service, or your chosen provider. Nothing is sent when you don't ask. If you pick a model on your own PC (Ollama or LM Studio), it stays on your PC.
  • The app talks to GitHub. It checks for new versions, and downloads updates, the game-data file and addon updates from there. These are plain downloads. They don't carry your character.
  • Nothing else goes over the network.

Copy diagnostics in Settings > Updates puts a short summary on your clipboard for you to paste when you report a problem. It holds versions and recent errors, with character names, your user folder, keys and email addresses taken out. It is made when you click, and the app never saves or sends it.

Each AI service has its own privacy rules. Read the one for the AI you choose. See AI options.

Where your settings and keys are kept

  • API keys go in Windows Credential Manager, the place Windows keeps passwords. They are never written to a settings file or a log, and the app never shows a saved key again. In Settings > AI you can Replace or Remove it any time.
  • Other settings (how the app looks, your play style, which AI you picked) are kept in the app's own folders in your Windows user profile. Your choice of provider is saved without any key.
  • The downloaded game-data file is kept in your user profile too. Deleting it only makes the app download it again.

Open source

The code is open under the MIT license, so anyone can read it: the app and the addon. The game data is open too, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
